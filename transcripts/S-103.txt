People often ask how our platform group grows its team. In this talk I explain the hiring process from the first outreach message to the final offer. We talk about writing a role description that reflects the actual day to day work, designing interview sessions that test collaboration rather than trivia, and training interviewers so feedback stays consistent across candidates. I also cover how we run the debrief meeting, how we try to avoid bias in the final decision, and how we onboard a new colleague during the first ninety days. Later we discuss the follow-up survey we send to every candidate, how we read the results, and the small changes we made after last year's feedback. We close with a question and answer session about growing a healthy team culture without burning out the interviewers.The goal is a process that feels fair to candidates and genuinely useful to the team.
