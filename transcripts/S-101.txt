Welcome to this session on scaling neural networks for production workloads. Today we walk through the core decisions a team faces when a research prototype has to serve real traffic. We begin with data pipelines, because a model is only as good as the examples that feed it. Next we discuss training strategy: how to choose batch sizes, how learning rates interact with the schedule, and why checkpointing matters when a single run can last for many days. We then move to evaluation, covering offline metrics and the surprising gaps that appear once a model meets live users. Finally we cover serving: batching requests, caching embeddings, monitoring drift, and rolling back a model quickly when quality drops. Along the way we share lessons from several teams that moved from a notebook to a production service without losing the ability to experiment.
