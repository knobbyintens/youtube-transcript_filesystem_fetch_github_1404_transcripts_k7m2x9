Modern authentication is only as strong as the encryption underneath it. In this session we review how credentials travel across the network, why transport encryption matters, and what happens when a token leaks. We walk through a realistic vulnerability report, showing how an attacker can replay a session and how a threat model helps us spot the gap before it ships. We then cover practical hardening: rotating keys, shortening token lifetimes, using constant time comparison, and logging authentication failures without leaking secrets. We also review how signing algorithms are chosen, why key rotation needs a documented owner, and how to verify that old sessions are revoked after a password reset. A short demo shows how a misconfigured setting exposes tokens in logs, and how a simple review catches it before an attacker notices. We close with a checklist teams can apply the same afternoon, plus a short discussion of how to test encryption settings in staging before they reach production.
